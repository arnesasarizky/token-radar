Which protocols are worth the most
before they have a token?

Every protocol here still records no token of its own. The radar asks what a token would be worth if it existed today — and ranks the board by the answer. The model is one line:

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × 7-day growth factor × tokenless-age factor

The peer multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of token-bearing protocols in the same category — what the market already pays for a dollar of TVL in comparable protocols — shrunk toward the global median (0.169× across 144 peers) when a category has few peers of its own, and clamped between 0.02× and 2.5×. The growth factor turns seven days of TVL change into a 0.5–1.8 multiplier; the age factor adds 6% for every year a protocol has run without a token, up to +35%. Nothing here predicts a token will arrive: the value is the model's claim, stated so it can be checked.

Where the multiples come from

A pre-token protocol is valued against protocols whose tokens already trade. For each category the model takes the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of its token-bearing peers (TVL $5M or more), then shrinks it toward the global median so a category with two peers cannot shout down one with twenty. Categories with fewer than three peers borrow the global median outright. These are the multiples actually applied to the board:

How to read this board: rank is the model's ordering by implied value, nothing else. A protocol can be large, old and still cheap if its category's tokens trade at a low multiple of TVL; a small one can rank high on momentum alone. Every farm plan is honest use of the product — deposit, trade, lend, hold — because a launch, if one ever comes, can only reward wallets the protocol can actually see. No step promises a token, an airdrop or a return.
